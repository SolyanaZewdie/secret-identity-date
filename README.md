# Secret Identity Date

Build a polished, high-fidelity, mobile-first web prototype for a couples’ date experience called LELA.

1. BRAND

Name

LELA

The name is inspired by the Amharic word ሌላ (lela), meaning “another / other.”

The idea behind the name is:

Be someone else tonight.

LELA is about giving couples permission to step outside their normal selves for one date and create a memorable experience together.

Main tagline

Be someone else tonight.

Alternative supporting line:

Your date. Their secret. A different you.

2. WHAT LELA IS

LELA is a playful couples’ experience that transforms an ordinary real-world date into something unexpected.

Before their date, each partner privately receives a character or persona.

They do NOT know what character their partner received.

They then meet in person for a normal date — dinner, coffee, a walk, an activity, etc. — while dressing, speaking, and behaving like their assigned character.

The important part:

They are still themselves.

They are not expected to become professional actors.

The character simply gives them:

A personality

A way of speaking

Mannerisms

An outfit/style direction

A few behavioral guidelines

Sometimes a starter line

Sometimes a small scenario

Sometimes optional missions

The characters do NOT have to complement each other.

They can be completely random.

Examples:

Partner A:
🧪 The Eccentric Chemist

Partner B:
✈️ The Overconfident Pilot

OR:

Partner A:
🤠 The Cowboy

Partner B:
🎨 The Famous Artist

OR:

Partner A:
🧛 The Vampire

Partner B:
☕ The Exhausted Barista

The randomness is intentional.

3. WHAT LELA IS NOT

This distinction is extremely important.

LELA is NOT:

A dating app

A matchmaking app

Couples therapy

A relationship advice app

Primarily a competitive game

A board game

A complicated RPG

A platform where couples spend their entire date staring at their phones

The actual date happens in the real world.

LELA simply adds an unexpected layer of personality, imagination, and playfulness to the date.

The app should encourage users to put their phones away once the date starts.

4. THE CORE EXPERIENCE

The core experience is:

Create a date → secretly receive a character → prepare → meet in person → stay in character → reveal yourselves → remember the experience.

The most important moment is:

You don't know who your partner is going to be until you meet them.

The app should make this secrecy feel exciting.

The actual date experience should be extremely easy to use on a phone.

18. SCENARIO SYSTEM

Scenarios are OPTIONAL.

Some dates should have no scenario whatsoever.

For a simple character date:

That's it.

“Tonight, you're simply being someone else.”

For other dates, provide a lightweight scenario.

Examples:

Scenario: The Secret Meeting

“You've both been asked to meet someone at this restaurant. You don't know that the other person received the same assignment.”

Scenario: The Wrong Identity

“You're both pretending to be someone you're not. Neither of you knows the other's real identity.”

Scenario: The Suspicious Stranger

“You have a feeling your partner knows something you don't.”

Scenarios should enhance the character experience, not turn the date into a complicated game.

25. IMPORTANT CHARACTER REVEAL MECHANIC

The character reveal must remain private.

The prototype should simulate this even without a backend.

Partner A receives a random character.

Partner B receives a separate random character.

The prototype must ensure they are different.

The user should be able to experience the flow as:

Partner A → private reveal → hide screen → hand phone to Partner B → Partner B → private reveal.

Make this interaction very obvious.
